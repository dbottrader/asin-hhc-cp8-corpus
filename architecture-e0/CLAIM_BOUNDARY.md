# Claim Boundary

## Permitted claims

This package may be described as:

- a Hugging Face-compatible CP8 architecture specification;
- an E0 research artifact;
- a reproducible target for constructing a GPT-2-class model;
- a provenance-first staging package for future tokenizer, dataset, and checkpoint releases.

## Prohibited claims

This package must not be described as:

- the trained CP8 model;
- a fine-tuned CP8 checkpoint;
- a validated geometric reasoning model;
- an independently reproduced scientific result;
- evidence of emergent behavior;
- an inference-ready model.

## Promotion rule

A claim may be promoted only when the supporting files and immutable receipts are included, hashed, and independently reproducible.

**No mechanism may silently convert uncertainty into authority.**
